Questions bakeries & cafés ask.

Can customers order custom cakes through the site?

Yes. The order form asks for the date, size, flavor and any notes, and sends it straight to you. You confirm the price and deposit the way you already do.

Can I show photos of cakes I've made?

Yes. Send us your best photos and we build a gallery. The Loaded plan also includes a photo session at your shop.

Can you add holiday pre-orders?

Yes. We add a pre-order page before the season and take it down after. On the Super that counts as your monthly update.

We sell out early. Can the site say so?

Yes. We can add a line like "Conchas usually gone by 11" right next to your hours, which also saves you phone calls.
