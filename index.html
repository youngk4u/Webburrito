Questions owners ask.

Do I need to know anything about computers?

No. We set up everything: the website, the .com, the email. If you can open a text message, you can see your site.

What do I need to give you?

Nothing to start. We build the preview from your public listing. If you like it, send us a few photos of your shop and your menu or price list, and we'll swap them in.

What if I don't like the preview?

Tell us what to change and we'll change it. If it's still not for you, you owe nothing.

Who owns the website and the .com?

You do. The .com is registered in your name. If you ever leave, we hand over the domain and the site files.

Can you change my menu or hours later?

Yes. Send a text or a photo of the change. Updates are included in every plan.

Can the site be in Spanish, Tagalog or Korean?

Yes. The Super and the Loaded include a second language. On the Regular it's a $99 add-on.

How do I pay?

Card, Zelle, check or cash for the setup. The yearly fee can be paid once a year, and we remind you before every renewal.

How long does it take?

Your preview is usually ready in a few days. Once you approve it, your site goes live within the week.
