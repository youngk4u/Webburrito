This explains what Web Burrito collects when you use webburrito.com, why, and who helps us run it. Questions or requests: hello@webburrito.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and name, from Google sign-in or the sign-in link we email you.
  • Your business details and site: what you type in, the changes you ask for, and the site that's built from them.
  • Domain owner details: the name, address, email and phone you give for your domain. Domain registration rules require these, and they're shared with our registrar. They're hidden from the public listing where that's possible.
  • Payments: handled by Square. We never see or store your card number; we keep the card type and last four digits so you can tell which card is on file.
  • Preview visits: when someone opens a preview link we sent, we record that it was opened, the type of device and browser, the time zone, and how long they looked, under a random ID. No cookies, and no IP addresses are stored.
  • Free preview requests: what you type into the form on the home page.

How we use it

To build, check, host and update your site; to register and renew your domain; to bill you; and to contact you about your account. We don't sell your information or use it for ads.

Who helps us

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, our database, and webburrito.com hosting.
  • Anthropic (Claude): the AI that builds and checks your site. It receives your business details and site to do that.
  • Square: payments.
  • Cloudflare: domain registration, hosting customer sites, and email forwarding.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your information while you have an account. To get a copy or have it deleted, email us; we'll do it within 30 days, except what we must keep for tax and payment records.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll email you first.
