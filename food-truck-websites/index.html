Questions food trucks ask.

My location changes every day. How does the site keep up?

We connect a Google Calendar or your Instagram feed to the site. When you update those, the site updates too. You don't have to touch the website.

Can people book my truck for events?

Yes. The event form asks for the date, headcount, location and budget, and sends it to you by text or email.

Do I need a website if I have Instagram?

Instagram is great for regulars. Event planners and new customers usually start with a Google search, and a site with your menu and a booking form is what turns that search into a booking.

Can the menu change with the season?

Yes. Text us a photo of the new menu and we update it. The Loaded plan includes unlimited small updates.
