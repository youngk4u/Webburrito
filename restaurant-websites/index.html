Questions restaurants ask.

Can you put my whole menu online?

Yes. Send a photo of your menu board or paper menu and we type it in. When prices change, text us a photo of the new ones.

Do I need online ordering?

No. Plenty of restaurants do fine with tap-to-call and directions. If you already use Square, Toast or Clover, the Loaded plan links straight to it.

Will a website help me show up on Google Maps?

Your Google listing is what shows on Maps. Every plan links your site to it and fixes your hours, and the Super and the Loaded set it up fully with photos and services. A real website gives Google more to trust, but nobody can honestly promise a ranking.

What about DoorDash and Uber Eats?

We can link to them. Your own site and phone number also let regulars order from you directly, without the app fees.
