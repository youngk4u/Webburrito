Questions salons & barbers ask.

I already use Vagaro or Booksy. Do I still need a website?

Your booking page lists you next to every other salon on that app. Your own site is the one place that's only about you, and it's what shows up when someone searches your salon's name.

Can I show photos of my work?

Yes. Send us your favorites or point us to your Instagram, and we build a gallery.

Can each stylist have their own page?

Yes. On the Super you get up to 3 pages, and the Loaded has unlimited pages, so every stylist or barber can have one with their own booking link.

Can clients book with a specific stylist?

If your booking app supports it, each stylist's Book button goes straight to their calendar.
